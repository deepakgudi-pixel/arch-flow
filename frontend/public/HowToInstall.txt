HOW TO INSTALL ARCHFLOW ON MAC (Unsigned Version)
-----------------------------------------------

Because this app is currently in development and not yet signed by Apple, your Mac will initially block it. 

Follow these steps to open it:

1. Drag "Archflow" to your Applications folder as usual.
2. If it says "App is damaged", open your TERMINAL and run:
   sudo xattr -cr /Applications/Archflow.app
3. Now, RIGHT-CLICK the Archflow app and select "Open".
4. A warning will appear. Click "Open" again. (This only needs to be done once).

If "Open" doesn't appear:
1. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security.
2. Scroll down to the "Security" section.
3. You will see a message saying "Archflow was blocked...".
4. Click "Open Anyway".

Enjoy designing!
